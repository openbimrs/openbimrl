# Documentation instructions

Documentation must preserve the repository's narrow reservation scope.

- Do not imply that an authoritative openBIMRL specification has been found.
- Do not claim parser, language, schema, serialization, validation, or
  conformance behavior.
- Do not imply endorsement by or affiliation with a standards body, industry
  group, or other organization.
- Keep package architecture synchronized with manifests and the semantic alias
  gate.
- Keep standards and other third-party artifacts out of the repository.
- Update README, architecture, rustdoc, and CHANGELOG together for public
  contract changes.
